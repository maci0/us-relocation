Method, sources and caveats

  • Scoring: each criterion is min-max scaled to 0–100 across the 22 places, flipped where lower is better. The overall score is the weighted average. Beach distance uses a square-root curve: the first 50 miles count most, and anything past 400 mi scores 0. Climate counts degrees above an 88°F July high plus degrees below a 35°F January low. Convenience is 60% Walk Score of the family area and 40% metro size (log).
  • Money left vs Singapore: yearly US take-home on $257.9k (MFJ, 1 child), minus the full family budget (rent, 2 cars, groceries, eating out, utilities, healthcare, Waldorf/kindergarten), plus the 401(k) match. That's compared with Singapore savings of about $121k on S$330k (EP, no CPF). Taken from opportunity_cost.py / section 08. Every place is negative, so a higher score means "least worse off".
  • House price: Zillow ZHVI (Sep 2026) for the family area shown (e.g. Frisco, Cary, Lehi), not the core city. Everyday prices: BEA Regional Price Parity 2024.
  • Crime: FBI UCR / city police data, per 100k, for the same family area. Several suburb figures come via sites that republish FBI data (OpenCrime, AreaVibes). Jacksonville uses Duval city rates because there's no reliable St. Johns figure. Orlando's property crime is estimated.
  • Beach: straight-line miles to the nearest sea coast, computed from the Natural Earth 1:50m coastline (Great Lakes excluded). Where that point is a bay or tidal river (Potomac, Pamlico Sound, Galveston Bay, Tampa Bay, SF Bay), the distance to the nearest real ocean beach is used instead (estimated). Seattle's 2 mi is Puget Sound salt water; the Pacific beaches are about 110 mi away. Phoenix's nearest sea is the Gulf of California (Mexico).
  • Driving: traffic is INRIX 2025 Global Traffic Scorecard, hours lost per driver. Places outside INRIX's US top 25 (under 41 h) are estimated and marked est. Road safety is IIHS 2024 deaths per 100M vehicle miles, by state. Snow is the NOAA 1991–2020 normal annual snowfall, rounded.
  • Living standards: schools are Niche district grades turned into 0–100 (A+ = 97). Life expectancy is the County Health Rankings county figure (approximate, ±1 yr). Healthcare is my 1–5 rating of major academic or cancer centres nearby (e.g. Mayo Jacksonville, MD Anderson Houston, Duke/UNC Raleigh).
  • Politics: 2024 presidential Trump share. Unofficial Nov 2026 results: Democrats won the TX and GA governorships and flipped the NC House and both Arizona chambers, and the Utah GOP lost its House supermajority. FL, TN, SC and ID stayed firmly Republican.
  • Travel: international airport = nonstop international destinations from the metro's main long-haul airport (San Jose uses SFO, NYC uses JFK, Naperville uses O'Hare), scored on a log scale so 100 vs 120 matters less than 0 vs 20. Domestic airport is a 1–22 rank by nonstop reach. Red Hat office = an office within commuting distance (Raleigh HQ, Atlanta, Plano, N. Charleston, Santa Clara, Boston, Tysons).
  • New in Nov 2026 (full tables and sources in the research document, section 11): cost of owning = property tax after homestead + home insurance + expected flood cost + HOA for the typical home (Texas MUD/PID fees not included, add $2–6k); 4-bed home for $400–600k, dogs (breed bans, insurer breed rules, HOA limits, heat), founder fit and startup ecosystem are 1–5 researcher ratings from sourced figures; Waldorf = schools within ~30 min (capped at 4) + 3 if a tuition-free public Waldorf charter is in reach; school money = state ESA/voucher per child in 2026–27; Texas is counted at half because the governor-elect has pledged to end it, and several states give a ~$260k household low priority; community = JEC county Social Capital Index; momentum = average of scaled net domestic migration (2025), IRS net income moving in per person and 12-month job growth; reliable power = 2024 utility outage minutes (log scale; 2024 hurricanes inflate Houston, Tampa, Greenville, Charleston); homelessness = HUD 2025 count per 10k (log scale); Republican-run state from 2027 = 1 for a Republican governor and legislature, 0.33 for TX, GA and NC (Democratic governor and House, Republican Senate), 0 for Democratic control.
  • Hazard: FEMA National Risk Index county rating (1 = very low, 5 = very high). Florida home insurance ($2.8–6k/yr) isn't in the house price.
